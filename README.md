Privacy Policy for SOPSC

Privacy Policy Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SirMrTyler Tech Solutions LLC ("the Company," "We," "Us," or "Our") collects, uses, and shares information when You use the SOPSC mobile application and the related API (together, the "Service").

SOPSC is a tool for chaplains, first responders, and other authorized members of participating divisions. It supports account approval, incident reports, prayer requests, messages, news, schedules, notifications, and in-app tutorials.

We use Personal Data to operate that Service. By creating an account or using the Service, You agree to this Privacy Policy.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation Words with an initial capital letter have the meanings below. The same meaning applies in the singular and the plural.

Definitions

Account means the unique account created for You to access the Service. A new account stays pending until it is confirmed. Pending accounts do not receive an app session.

Application means the SOPSC mobile app for Android and iOS, provided by the Company.

Company means SirMrTyler Tech Solutions LLC, 5283 Nopal St., Oregon, United States.

Device means a phone or tablet that can access the Service.

Personal Data means information that relates to an identified or identifiable person. In this Service that includes Account data and, when a report or message contains it, information about people named in those records.

Service means the Application and the SOPSC API that supports it.

Service Provider means a person or company that processes data for Us so We can run the Service. Our Service Providers are listed under "Who We Share Data With."

Usage Data means data generated by use of the Service, such as device identifiers, platform, push tokens, and server request logs.

You means the person using the Service, or the organization on whose behalf that person uses it.

Information We Collect

Account data When You register with email and password, or when an administrator creates or updates Your account, We collect:

First name and last name
Email address
Password, stored only as a bcrypt hash (email accounts)
The division You ask to join, and an optional note to the reviewer
Role and division memberships after approval
Phone number, if You add one on Your profile
Profile photo, if You choose one
A Firebase user id, used to connect Your account to messaging and notifications
Google sign-in is the only third-party login. If You sign in with Google, We receive the profile information Google provides for that sign-in, including Your name, email address, and profile photo. We do not receive Your Google contacts. We do not offer sign-in through Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, or other social networks.

Profile photo Changing Your photo asks for access to Your photo library. We upload the image You select to Our servers and store its path on Your Account. We do not access Your camera, and We do not read Your photo library beyond the image You pick.

Information You enter while using the Service Depending on Your role, the Service stores content You or other members submit:

Incident reports. Division, agencies, type of service, dispatch dates and times, the contact’s name, phone, and email, the dispatch address, and a narrative. Reports can describe real people and incidents. Members see their own reports. Division managers see reports for their division. Admins and developers see reports across divisions.
Prayer requests and comments. Subject, body, author name, and prayer counts. These are visible to other signed-in users.
Messages. Direct and group chat content, membership, and read state. A conversation is visible to its participants. Messages may be stored in Our database and, when that feature is enabled, in Google Cloud Firestore.
Schedule. Calendar events and category assignments. When Google Calendar is configured for the Service, event details may also be sent to Google Calendar.
News and notifications. Announcements and the notification text sent to Your account.
Tutorials. Which required and library modules You have been assigned and completed.
We do not collect Your device’s GPS location. A dispatch address exists only when someone types it into a report. We do not read Your contacts or phone book.

Device and session data When You use the Service We store:

A device identifier and the platform (such as iOS or Android)
An Expo push token, if You allow notifications
Session credentials. The app keeps an access token and a refresh token in the device’s secure storage. Our servers store only a hash of the refresh token.
Our servers receive the requests Your device sends. Those logs can include Your IP address, the time of the request, and the API route called. We use that information to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

How We Use Personal Data

We use Personal Data to:

Create and approve Accounts, sign You in, and keep Your session current
Enforce roles so each person sees only the divisions, reports, messages, and admin tools their role allows
Store and show reports, prayer requests, messages, news, and schedule events
Send account email, including registration mail, through Our email provider
Send push notifications about activity in the Service, if You allow them
Show Your name and profile photo to other members who interact with You
Keep audit records of administrative actions, including access changes and account removals
Protect the Service, investigate misuse, and meet legal obligations
We do not sell Personal Data. We do not use it for third-party advertising, and We do not send promotional offers about unrelated products.

Who We Share Data With

Other users. Other members can see information the Service is built to show them: Your name and photo in messages and directories, prayer requests You post, and reports their role permits them to open. Chat content is limited to participants in that chat.

Service Providers. We share only what each provider needs to perform its function:

Google, to validate Google sign-in and, when configured, to write calendar events
Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Messaging), when configured, for conversations and message delivery
Expo, to deliver push notifications to Your device
Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), to send transactional account email
Our host and database operator, to store Account and Service data in Microsoft SQL Server and to run the API
Administrators of participating organizations. Admins, developers, and division managers use the Service to review join requests, removal requests, report reviews, and user records within their authority.

Business transfers. If the Company is involved in a merger, sale, financing, or similar transfer, Personal Data may be part of that transfer. We will provide notice before it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Legal and safety. We may disclose Personal Data if We believe in good faith that disclosure is required to comply with law, respond to a valid request from a public authority, protect the rights or safety of users or the public, investigate misuse of the Service, or establish or defend a legal claim.

We may also disclose Personal Data when You ask Us to.

Retention

We keep Personal Data for as long as Your Account is active and as long as needed to operate the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal duties.

If an administrator removes Your Account, We may still retain incident reports, the admin audit log, the removal record, and tombstoned messages. Those records can remain visible to administrators. Other profile and session data is removed or disconnected according to the removal process.

You may update Your name, phone, profile photo, and primary division in the app. To request access, correction, or deletion beyond what the app allows, email Us. We may decline to delete information We must keep for operations, safety, or law.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Session refresh tokens are stored as hashes. The app keeps tokens in device secure storage, and the API expects HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Service is for chaplains, first responders, and other authorized adult members of participating organizations. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from anyone under 13. If You believe a child under 13 has given Us Personal Data, contact Us and We will delete it.

Links

The Service may link to sites We do not operate, including Google. Their privacy policies govern those sites.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version, change the "Last updated" date, and, when the change is material, notify You by email or by a notice in the Service. The new policy applies when it is posted.
